Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  Effective immediately

📥 Data We Collect

  • Images you upload for scanning — processed in memory, never stored on our servers.
  • Scan results (verdict, confidence, flags) — stored in your personal Firestore account if you are signed in.
  • A 100×100 pixel thumbnail of each scanned image — stored only in your own Firestore account, visible only to you.
  • Your Google account display name, email, and profile photo — used only to show your avatar in the app.
  • Language preference — saved locally and optionally synced to your Firestore account.

🚫 Data We Don't Collect

  • We do not store your original full-resolution images anywhere.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetise any of your data.
  • We do not track you across other websites or apps.
  • We do not use your images to train AI models.

⚙️ How We Use Your Data

  • Images are sent to our backend (FastAPI + Google Gemini) solely to produce an authenticity verdict.
  • Scan history is stored in Firebase Firestore under your unique user ID, accessible only to you (enforced by Firestore Security Rules).
  • A global scan counter (no personal data) is incremented after each scan and displayed as social proof.

🔑 Your Rights

  • Export — download a JSON file of all your scan results at any time from Settings → Account.
  • Delete — delete your account and all associated data instantly from Settings → Account. This action is irreversible.
  • Guest use — you may use Asli without signing in; no data is stored in that case.
